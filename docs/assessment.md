# Assessment

---

## Breakdown

| Component | Weight | Timing |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Assignments (8)** | **20%** | Weeks 2, 4, 6, 9, 10, 12, 14, 16 |
| **Tests (2)** | **30%** | Week 8 (15%) + Week 15 (15%) |
| **Final examination** | **50%** | Week 18 |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

---

## Assignments (20%)

Each assignment covers one chapter. Format: a PDF report plus source code.

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|:---:|
| Correctness of answers | 40% |
| Rigour of reasoning | 20% |
| Implementation | 20% |
| Interpretation | 15% |
| Presentation | 5% |

**Late policy:** 10% per calendar day; zero after 3 days.

---

## Tests (30%)

Two tests, each 1.5 hours, closed book.

- **Test 1 (week 8, 15%)** — Chapters 1–3.
- **Test 2 (week 15, 15%)** — Chapters 4–7.

---

## Final examination (50%)

- **Format.** 3 hours, closed book.
- **Content.** Whole syllabus, weighted toward Chapters 4–9 (the applied material).
- **Structure.** 6–8 questions, mixed theory and computation.

## Academic integrity

All submitted work must be your own. Collaboration on *understanding* is encouraged; collaboration on *submitted answers* is plagiarism.
